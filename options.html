Settings

Appearance

Layout

Automatic fits up to 7 × 5 icons, like Launchpad. Over 3,000 well-known sites get their brand logo; a picture or emoji you pick always wins.

Wallpaper

Your own image

Photo of the day is Wikimedia Commons' picture of the day, downloaded once a day.

Widgets

Weather comes from Open-Meteo: only the city you type is sent, never your location. Frequent sites are Chrome's own most-visited list, read on this computer.

Bookmarks

Folders on the home grid

Everything in your Bookmarks bar is always shown. Choose which of Chrome's other top-level folders appear too. Bookmarks, folders and the Dock are Chrome bookmarks, so Chrome already syncs them.

Privacy & Integrations

Next meeting from Google Calendar
Sync icons and wallpaper with Google Drive

Calendar: only today's upcoming events are read, on this computer, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere except Google.

Drive: your chosen bookmark icons and your own wallpaper image are kept in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, so they follow you to every computer where you connect. The extension can't see anything else in your Drive. To remove the file and the access everywhere, go to your Google Account's third-party access page.

Maintenance

Duplicate bookmarksThe same page saved more than once
Broken linksPages that are gone or can't be reached

Checking links asks for access to websites just for the check, and gives it back when done.

Backup & Restore

Settings, tasks and notesSave them to a file, or restore from one

Restoring first downloads a copy of what you have now, so you can undo it by restoring that file.

Changes and tasks save straight away and sync to other computers signed in to Chrome. Your own wallpaper image and bookmark icons stay on this computer unless Google Drive sync is on.
